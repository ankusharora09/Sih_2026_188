# Dataset Notes & Licensing

This project relies on several publicly available research datasets. Below are brief notes on each dataset’s purpose, licensing constraints, and any required attribution.

---

## MIDV‑500
- **Purpose:** Document detection, classification, and OCR benchmarking for identity documents (passports, IDs, driver licences).
- **License:** Research‑only, non‑commercial. Requires citation of the original paper.
- **Usage:** Allowed for model development and benchmarking within Phase 1–4. Must not be redistributed commercially.
- **Location in repo:** `data/raw/midv500/` (git‑ignored). The repo includes a small subset for demonstration only.

---

## MIDV‑2019
- **Purpose:** Provides harder images (blur, low‑light) to stress‑test preprocessing and OCR.
- **License:** Similar research‑only restrictions as MIDV‑500.
- **Usage:** Optional; can complement MIDV‑500 for robustness testing.

---

## PaddleOCR / Tesseract
- **Purpose:** Primary OCR engines. PaddleOCR offers multilingual, neural‑network‑based OCR; Tesseract serves as a classical fallback.
- **License:** Apache‑2.0 (PaddleOCR) and Apache‑2.0 (Tesseract). Commercial use permitted.
- **Usage:** Free to integrate; ensure model files are bundled or downloaded at runtime (Phase 4).

---

## OpenCV
- **Purpose:** Image preprocessing, quality analysis, perspective correction, and feature extraction.
- **License:** BSD‑3‑Clause. Free for commercial and research use.

---

## DocTamper
- **Purpose:** Research dataset for document tampering detection (synthetic manipulations, compression artefacts).
- **License:** Non‑commercial research use only; redistribution is prohibited without permission.
- **Usage:** Allowed for benchmarking tampering detectors in Phase 4. Must include attribution in any published results.

---

## CelebA‑Spoof
- **Purpose:** Large‑scale anti‑spoofing dataset containing live and spoofed face images (photo, video, printed, digital attacks).
- **License:** Non‑commercial research use. Requires acknowledgment of the dataset creators.
- **Usage:** Training/evaluating liveness detection models (Phase 4+). No commercial deployment without additional licensing.

---

## FaceForensics++
- **Purpose:** Benchmark for deep‑fake detection, containing manipulated videos generated by various synthesis methods.
- **License:** Non‑commercial research; redistribution of the raw media is restricted.
- **Usage:** Evaluate deep‑fake detection stage (Phase 4+). Must store a copy locally under `data/raw/faceforensics/` (git‑ignored) and cite the dataset.

---

## OULU‑NPU (optional)
- **Purpose:** Additional anti‑spoofing benchmark with varied illumination and attack types.
- **License:** Research‑only, similar to CelebA‑Spoof.
- **Usage:** Optional for extended liveness evaluation.

---

## InsightFace / ArcFace
- **Purpose:** State‑of‑the‑art face detection and embedding models for verification and similarity search.
- **License:** MIT (InsightFace). Commercial use permitted provided attribution.
- **Usage:** Used for generating face embeddings in the verification stage.

---

## FAISS / pgvector
- **Purpose:** Vector similarity search for face‑reuse detection and fast nearest‑neighbor queries.
- **License:** MIT (FAISS), PostgreSQL license (pgvector). Free for commercial use.
- **Usage:** Planned for Phase 4+ to implement fast duplicate‑face detection.

---

## Redis
- **Purpose:** In‑memory store for rate‑limiting counters, velocity windows, and temporary session data.
- **License:** BSD‑3‑Clause. Free for commercial use.
- **Usage:** Optional in Phase 2 as a lightweight cache; may be introduced later for anomaly detection.

---

## Hyperledger Fabric (future)
- **Purpose:** Permissioned blockchain for tamper‑evident audit anchoring.
- **License:** Apache‑2.0. Commercially usable.
- **Usage:** Not part of Phase 1; design documented for future integration (see `docs/AUDIT_BLOCKCHAIN.md`).

---

### Licensing Checklist for Phase 1

- No dataset files are committed to the repository (all `data/raw/` directories are listed in `.gitignore`).
- All non‑commercial datasets are flagged with usage restrictions; the repository includes only small placeholder samples for illustration.
- Attribution statements are prepared for inclusion in any future publications or demo videos.

---

*When moving to Phase 2‑5, ensure that any code that loads or distributes these datasets respects the licensing terms. Synthetic data generated in‑house is fully owned by the project and may be used without restriction.*
